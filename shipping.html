Shipping & delivery

A clear overview of what happens after you send your order request.

Editable store value: shipping charges and delivery estimates are confirmed before order acceptance. Update this in BROFIT_CONFIG.shippingNote.

Order confirmation

A WhatsApp order request does not become a confirmed order until BRO FIT verifies the product, size, quantity, delivery details and payment method with you.

Order processing

After confirmation, we prepare and quality-check the item. We will contact you if any detail needs clarification or if availability changes.

Shipping

The available shipping method and applicable charge are shared before the order is accepted. These values can vary by destination and order size.

Delivery areas

Delivery coverage is checked using the address or postal code you provide. Contact us to confirm whether your location is currently serviceable.

Estimated delivery

The estimate is shared after confirming availability and destination. It is an estimate, not a guaranteed delivery date, and may change due to carrier conditions.

Customer contact process

Keep the mobile number used for the order reachable. BRO FIT or the delivery partner may contact you for address confirmation or delivery coordination.
