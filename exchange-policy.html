Return & exchange policy

Simple guidance for size concerns, damaged items and eligible exchanges.

Editable store value: replace “Update the eligible exchange period here” in BROFIT_CONFIG.exchangeWindow before launch.

Exchange eligibility

Contact BRO FIT within the current eligible exchange period and wait for approval before sending or bringing back a product.

Size exchange

A size exchange depends on stock availability. Share your order details, product code and requested size through WhatsApp.

Product condition

Items must be unworn, unwashed, unused and returned with original tags and packaging. Products showing wear, odour, alteration or damage after delivery may not qualify.

Damaged or incorrect item

Contact us promptly with clear photos or video of the package and product. Keep the item and packaging until we review the issue.

Non-exchangeable conditions

Items may be ineligible if they are outside the published period, used, washed, altered, missing tags, or part of a category clearly marked non-exchangeable before purchase.

Contact procedure

Message BRO FIT on WhatsApp with your name, mobile number, product code, order reference, reason and supporting photos where relevant. We will share the next step after review.
