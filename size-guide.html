Simple steps

How to measure

01 · Chest

Measure around the fullest part, keeping the tape level and relaxed.

02 · Shoulder

Measure straight across the back from one shoulder point to the other.

03 · Waist

Measure around where you naturally wear your trousers without pulling tight.

04 · Length

Measure from the top edge of the garment to the finished hem.
